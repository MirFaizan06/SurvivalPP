# The Chronicles of the Seven Seals - Lore

> **Spoiler warning.** This page tells the whole story that exists so far (Chapters I to VI) and the history behind it. If you want to discover it through the **Wayfarer's Journal** in game, stop reading here.

The add-on is a story-driven adventure. The journal you receive when you first join gives you one clue at a time; each clue sends you to a real place, a real fight and a real reward. This document is the long version.

## 1. Before the Eclipse

Long ago the world had **one sky** and **one dawn that never ended**. There was no Overworld, no Nether, no End - only the **Dawnlands**, a single bright country under a single patient sun. The First Dawn was not a star. It was the world's heartbeat: a light that remembered every living thing and made room for all of it.

Where that light fell hardest it hardened into crystal. People later called the crystal **Azurite** and thought it was only a pretty stone. It is the First Dawn, frozen where it fell.

## 2. The Hollow Sun

Nobody knows what the **Eclipse** was before it rose. The old songs call it the *Hollow Sun*: a second sun that gave no light, only a hunger for the first one. It rose over the Dawnlands and **swallowed the First Dawn whole**.

The world cracked.

- What stayed in the daylight became the **Overworld**.
- What fell into the Eclipse's heat became the **Burning Deep** (the Nether).
- What was thrown past the edge of the sky became the **Void Shore** (the End).

The Eclipse's power shattered with it - into **Seven Shards**. A Shard is a piece of the Hollow Sun: it wants to be whole again, and it whispers to anything that holds it. Two of the world's strange new materials are what the Shards left behind:

| Material | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Azurite** | The First Dawn, frozen into crystal. |
| **Pyrelite** | The Eclipse's blood, cooled in the Burning Deep. |
| **Eclipsite** | The Eclipse's shadow, turned to stone on the Void Shore. |

Carry all three and the Eclipse notices.

## 3. The Seven Seals and the Seven Keepers

Seven people refused to let the Shards be reunited. Each took one Shard and **bound it into a Seal** - a prison and a lock in one - and swore to guard it: the **Seven Keepers**.

A Seal is dangerous to keep. Break one carelessly and the Shard **bleeds into its Keeper**. The longer a Keeper holds a Seal alone, the more the Shard speaks with the Keeper's own voice. This is the central tragedy of the story: the guardians are slowly becoming the thing they guard.

| Seal | Keeper | Place | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **Hanae**, the Veiled Blossom | The Cherry Grove | Chapter I - **open** |
| II | **The Eclipse Sovereign** | The edge of the End | Chapter II - **open** |
| III | **Ysolde**, the Frost Cantor | The Frozen Cathedral, high in the snowy peaks | Road III - **open** |
| IV | **Morwen**, the Mire Lantern | The Drowned Chapel, deep in the fen | Road IV - **open** |
| V | **Varenna**, the Cinder Bride | The Ashen Chapel, in the Burning Deep | Road V - **open** |
| VI | **Corvane**, the Ashen Groom | The Hollow Hall, in Soul Sand Valley | Road VI - **open** |
| VII | *the ink is smeared* | ? | Unwritten |

## 4. The Hollow Court and the Hollow Lords

The Eclipse does not only hide. It **collects**.

Anything that dies near a Shard can be taught to serve a sun that gives no light. These are the **Hollow Court**:

- **Gravewalkers** - the buried, dragged back up in their burial shrouds.
- **Revenants** - soldiers who never got to stop fighting.
- **Cave Weavers** - the Silkqueen's unfinished lullabies, spun into spiders.
- **Sporelings** - the seeds a dying forest could no longer plant.

Above them stand three **Hollow Lords**. They are *not* Keepers and they hold *no* Seal. They are casualties: great beings the Eclipse broke and kept.

- **The Hollow King** - a mortal king who bargained with the Eclipse to cheat death. It kept its word: he cannot die, and he cannot rest. His court wanders the graveyards serving a throne that no longer exists. His relic, the **Grave Sigil**, was once a key to a Keeper's shrine.
- **The Mosswood Colossus** - the forest's last Greenwarden. When the Eclipse withered the sun its roots drank shadow instead, and the guardian woke wrong. Its relic is the **Verdant Heart**.
- **The Silkqueen** - the Mother of Threads, who spun webs to hold the cracking world together until the Eclipse taught her that everything caught is hers. Her relic is the **Silk Idol**.

The Hollow Lords are **side stories**: fight them for loot, relics and lore pages, but the road of the Seals does not pass through them.

## 5. The Wayfarer

Someone walked this road before you.

Her name has been scratched out of every page. She was a traveller who decided the Eclipse could be ended by finding every Seal, and she kept a journal as she went. The journal in your pack is hers. Its pages fill only as you walk where she walked - and the ink gets *shorter* after the second Seal. Her last legible line is about the Second Keeper: *"He knew my name."*

What happened to her is the oldest unanswered question in the story.

## 6. Chapter I - The Veiled Blossom

> *"Where pink snow never melts and the trees never stop blooming, the First Keeper sleeps. Walk beneath the blossoms. Listen."*

**Hanae** was the *miko* - the shrine maiden - of the Cherry Grove. She was the first Keeper to take a Shard, and she bound it with her own life: she wove a **veil of petals** over her face so that the Shard could not look out through her eyes, and she has kept it on ever since. She sees the Eclipse in every mirror.

She never wanted to be a boss. Over the years, travellers came to the grove - pilgrims, adventurers, people who wanted the Seal. Each time she tried to make them turn back. Each time they kept coming. The **Veiled Echoes** that follow her are the reflections of those she could not turn around.

Her message is the same for everyone who finds her: **the end of this road is impossible. Stop.**

### The road to her (what the journal asks of you)

| Step | Clue | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The First Whisper | Find a Cherry Grove. | Be where she can feel you. |
| 2. Tainted Roots | Slay 3 Gravewalkers and 2 Cave Weavers. | The Hollow Court has crept into her grove; cut it back so she will speak. |
| 3. Offerings | Present 16 Pink Petals, a Silk Gland, a Hollow Bone, a Spore Cluster. | A little of what each Hollow servant is made of. The offerings melt into the **Veiled Mirror** - she sees through glass, never through eyes. |
| 4. The Hidden Shrine | Find Hanae's Shrine and touch the Blossom Altar. | Torii gate, stone path, a pavilion of cherry wood and an altar of pink marble. |
| 5. The Veil | At night, in the grove, use the Mirror on the altar. | The mirror shatters. She comes. |

### The fight

She does not fight to kill. Her three phases are three ways of telling you to leave:

1. **The Veil.** She fans **petal blades** at you, **steps behind you** through the mirror, and splits into **Veiled Echoes** - reflections that shield her until you shatter them.
2. **Falling Blossoms.** The fog turns pink and thick. Petals fall in rings you can see coming; **Blossom Vexes** - little masked spirits of her grief - harry you.
3. **The Eclipse Bloom.** The Shard starts to speak through her. Her colours darken, the fog turns black-red, and the petals spiral outwards. She begs you to run while she can still stay her hand.

If you are badly hurt she **lowers her parasol and stops** until you recover. She would rather lose than kill.

> *"Turn back, wayfarer. I do not wish to hurt you."*
> *"Seven Seals... and the Eclipse waits behind the last."*
> *"No one who walked this road has ever returned."*
> *"I was like you once. I read the same journal."*
> *"The ending was never meant to be reached."*

When she falls, she gives up the Seal anyway:

> *"...You will not stop, will you? Then take my Seal, wayfarer. And please... do not let the Eclipse take you too."*
> *"...thank you. Six Seals remain."*

**Rewards:** the **Veilbloom Regalia** (a full set of pink armour as strong as netherite, with a Speed and Jump Boost set bonus), **Veilbloom Shards** (to repair it), the **Seal of the Blossom**, diamonds, an enchanted golden apple and a heap of Pink Petals.

## 7. Chapter II - The Second Seal

The journal's next entry is short and frightened:

> *"The First Seal is ours... but her plea will not leave me. The Second Keeper stands at the edge of the world - the Eclipse Sovereign, in the End. He knew my name when I found him."*

**The Eclipse Sovereign** was once the Keeper of the Void Shore. He stood at the edge of the world and **watched the Eclipse rise**. When the Shards scattered he did something none of the other Keepers dared: he **took the Second Shard into himself** to spare everyone else from it. It worked. It also made him the Eclipse's herald.

His **Voidweavers** are his unspoken doubts, given robes and spells. The **Eclipse Core** he leaves behind when he falls is the Shard's husk.

Defeating him completes Chapter II. How the Sovereign came to know the Wayfarer's name - and why the Wayfarer's journal gets thinner after him - is where the story is waiting.

## 8. Chapter III - The Silent Choir

> *"Hanae's plea was not the only voice in the grove. Under it, something hummed a lullaby I knew as a child."*

After the First Seal the Wayfarer's journal **forks**. She numbered the roads in the order she heard them calling: the Second (the End) because the Core hummed, and the Third and Fourth in the Overworld, because they were closest to home. A player can walk the Third before the Second; the Fourth only opens once the Third is done.

**Ysolde** was the **cantor** of the high snow. When the Shards scattered, the Third Shard fell into the mountains, and Ysolde did the only thing she knew how to do: she **sang to it**. The Shard is a hungry thing, but it is also an *infant* thing, and it sleeps while it is sung to. So she has been singing for longer than anyone remembers. The cold followed her song, and froze the world around her, so that it could listen.

The Choir's rule is simple and terrible: **a cantor sings so the Shard may sleep. The day the song ends, the Seal opens.** So the cantor may not rest, may not stop, and may not be thanked.

### The road to her

| Step | Clue | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Echoes in the Snow | Read the four **Verse Stones** (Dawn, Zenith, Dusk, Midnight) that stand in snowy lands. | The song is written in stone, one verse at a time. The **Cantor's Tuning Fork** the journal gives you hums towards them. |
| 2. The Sun's Road | Strike the four **chimes** of the **Frozen Cathedral** in the order of the sun's road. | The verses were never a song, they were a lock. Dawn, Zenith, Dusk, Midnight - but the chimes hang in a different order in space, so you have to *remember*. A wrong note is answered with frost. |
| 3. The Conductor | Strike the fork on the **Cantor's Lectern**. | The note does not fade. She comes. |

The cathedral is also home to a hidden vault (a cracked tile in the north-west corner), two spawners of the dead Choir, and a trap under the carpet.

### The fight

She conducts in **3/4 time**; every attack is announced by the beat.

1. **Overture.** The **Aria** (frozen notes), **Resonance Rings** (they only hurt those standing on the ground - jump) and the dead Choir answering.
2. **Crescendo.** The **Icicle Chorus** and the **Whiteout**: a blizzard that shoves you until you kneel.
3. **Requiem.** A double spiral of notes and the **Final Verse**, a three-second channel. Break it with enough damage and she is stunned; ignore it and a wave rolls out.

> *"Hush, wayfarer... you walk in the middle of a song."*
> *"She sang the last verse with me, once. The Wayfarer. She had a beautiful voice."*
> *"If the music stops, the Seal opens. If it never stops, I never rest."*

When she falls, she is quiet for the first time:

> *"...Do you hear that? It is silent. I had forgotten silence. Take my Seal, wayfarer - and sing the last verse for me, when you find her."*

**Rewards:** **Frostsong** (a conductor's sword; right-click for **Resonance**), the **Seal of the Choir**, Glacial Shards and Rime Shards, diamonds, an enchanted golden apple.

### The Eighth Voice

A tablet in the snow, in the Wayfarer's own hand, says that **eight** stood at the Cracking and **seven** took Shards. *"I took only the page."* The journal's ink is **Azurite dust** - the First Dawn, ground fine. Whoever the Wayfarer was, she wrote with the world's own morning. The Eclipse hungers for the First Dawn. The Wayfarer is *walking towards the Seals with it in her hands*. That is the question the rest of the story answers.

## 9. Chapter IV - The Lantern in the Mire

> *"Ysolde's last verse was a map, and it ended in a swamp. At night the water breathes little lights."*

**Morwen** was the **lamplighter** of the fen. She hung a lantern in every window so that no traveller would ever wander into the dark. When she took the Fourth Shard, the Shard learned her trick: the lights she hung still burn, but they now lead the other way. **Mire Wisps** are the travellers who followed the wrong light. A Seal that is a lantern which can never go out is a hard thing to carry.

### The road to her

| Step | Clue | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Lights in the Fen | Present **6 Wisp Essence** and a Lantern in the journal. | The essence is warm and remembers the way home. It becomes the **Keeper's Lantern**. |
| 2. The Drowned Chapel | Read the **Fen Notes**, then light the three **sconces** in the right order. | Half the riddle is in a hut in the fen; the other half is in a **flooded room** you reach by swimming down the chapel's pool. *The eye must watch before the star can guide; the moon leads home, and comes last.* A wrong order puts the fen's own lights out and wakes wisps. |
| 3. The Lantern Calls | At night, use the Keeper's Lantern on the **Marsh Altar**. | She only answers to a lit lantern, under a night sky. |

### The fight

The fen **swallows light**: every few seconds everyone who is not carrying a torch or lantern goes dark. The Keeper's Lantern lights everyone within ten blocks.

1. **Misdirection.** Foxfire bolts, wisp swarms, a lantern swing.
2. **Lost.** **Lantern Mirages**: she vanishes among decoys, and only the real one flickers with motes. A mirage that you strike takes your sight.
3. **Hollow Light.** **Lantern Rain** and a fog you cannot see through.

> *"Lost, are you? They always are. Follow the light - it never lies."*
> *"The Wayfarer carried a light like yours. She left half of it here, with me."*
> *"Put out my lantern, wayfarer. Please. Before it learns your name."*
> *"I only wanted someone to come home."*

When she falls:

> *"...It is quiet. The light is gone, and I am not afraid. Take my Seal, wayfarer. She is still walking ahead of you - I could see her lamp."*

**Rewards:** the **Mirelight Scythe** (sweeps and sips; right-click for the **Beacon of the Lost**), the **Seal of the Lantern**, Wisp Essence, diamonds, netherite scrap, an enchanted golden apple.

## 10. Chapter V - The Cinder Bride

> *"Morwen's lamp showed me a road down into the Burning Deep. There is a forge there where a wedding was interrupted at the vows."*

Road V opens when Road IV is done, and is the first road that leads **out of the Overworld**: it takes place in the Nether. **Varenna** was the smith-queen of the Burning Deep, and a bride. The night she was to be married, the Cracking came, the ceremony was interrupted at the vows (all three: **gold, flame and tears**), and nobody told the bride. She took the Fifth Shard and forged it into her **wedding ring**, so that it could not be taken from her hand without taking the hand. It was the only wedding ring in the Burning Deep, and the only thing she could think of that she would never put down. She has been standing at an anvil ever since, waiting for the word that finishes the sentence. Her hammer, her ring and her blades are the only language she has left.

### The road to her

| Step | Clue | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Vows Left Unsaid | Find the **Forge of Vows** and make the three offerings in the order the vow was spoken. | Three **pedestals** (flame, tears, gold - in space) wait for a **Gold Ingot**, a **Blaze Rod** and a **Ghast Tear**, **in that order**. The tablets tell you the order; the forge punishes anything out of turn (and returns what you offered). It gives you the **Cinder Ring** and the **Ember Compass**. |
| 2. The Bridal Anvil | Use the compass to find the **Ashen Chapel** and touch the **Bridal Anvil**. | A chapel of black carpet with one white thread, and an anvil where an altar should be. The ring has a twin. |
| 3. The Wedding | Set the Cinder Ring on the anvil. | It rings once, like a bell. She comes. |

### The fight (900 HP, four phases)

She is the first **harder** Keeper. Her fight is a forge:

* **Forged Blades.** She hammers out spectral swords that charge you. **While any blade lives she is shielded** - and every blade a *player* cuts down is a **parry**: it costs her 2.5% of her life. (Shielded, 80% of your damage is healed back.)
* **Anvil Slam** (telegraphed rings and ember geysers marked under your feet), **Cinder Brand** (a mark that, after five seconds, detonates on whoever stands near the branded player - *spread out*), **Forge Heat** (sectors of the floor glow, then burn; the safe sectors are the ones without embers) and **the Last Dance** (she crosses the floor and strikes from behind).
* In the last phase, **the Wedding March**: she never stops forging.

> *"You came to my wedding without an invitation. How rude... and how welcome."*
> *"Every blade I make is a promise I could not keep."*
> *"The Wayfarer stood where you stand. She knew his name before she knew mine."*
> *"Dance with me. The march has only one step."*

> *"...I can put the hammer down. Take my Seal, wayfarer - and when you find him, tell Corvane I kept the veil."*

**Rewards:** **Vowbreaker** (a forge hammer; ignites; right-click for an **Anvil Slam**), the **Seal of the Forge**, **Cinder Hearts** (fire resistance and strength), netherite scrap and ancient debris, diamonds, enchanted golden apples.

## 11. Chapter VI - The Ashen Groom

> *"Varenna asked me to tell him she kept the veil. The bells of Soul Sand Valley have been tolling since before I arrived."*

Road VI opens when Road V is done. **Corvane** was the **bell-ringer** of Soul Sand Valley, and the groom. When the Cracking took the wedding, he could not leave the valley, so he hung bells in a tower and tolled for every one who did not return. He took the Sixth Shard into his chest, as a flame, and swore to stand at the altar until she came. It did not occur to him that he might never be able to leave. **Her veil is on his arm. His ring is on her hand. They have been each other's Seals for longer than the valley has had a name.** To free one, the other must fall - the cruelest arithmetic of the Seals, and the Wayfarer found no way around it.

### The road to him

| Step | Clue | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Widower's Toll | Find the **Toll Tower** and ring back the pattern the bells toll, three rounds. | A memory game: the tower tolls a pattern (3, then 4, then 5 bells), and you ring it back. A wrong bell costs you; the tower falls silent and you start again. It drops the **Grave Key**. |
| 2. The Groom's Grave | Use the compass to find the **Hollow Hall** and touch the **Grave Altar**. | A hall of broken pews, a black aisle with a white thread, a ring of soul lanterns around an altar. |
| 3. The Last Vow | Set the Grave Key into the altar. | Every bell in the valley answers once. He comes. |

### The fight (1100 HP, four phases)

The hardest Keeper so far, and the fastest. His fight is a **duel of stances**; every few seconds he changes stance after a telegraph (the armour's glow changes colour):

* **Ember** (orange): immune to **projectiles and fire**. Use steel.
* **Soul** (blue): immune to **melee**. Use arrows, tridents, magic.
* **Hollow** (grey): takes everything, but he is **furious** - faster and harder-hitting.

Whatever his stance deflects is healed straight back. On top of that: the **Duel Lunge** (a telegraphed line he dashes along), **Soul Chains** (they drag you to him unless you break line of sight), **Marrow Rain**, **Grave Echoes** (wither skeletons) and the **Widow's Toll** - rings of bell-sound that roll out from him and that you must jump.

> *"You have her ring... no. Not hers. It only looks like hers."*
> *"Do you know what a widower is? A man who keeps his promises to someone who cannot hold him to them."*
> *"Tell her the ring still fits."*

> *"...I can hear the valley. It is only wind. Take my Seal, wayfarer. Tell her... no. I will tell her myself."*

**Rewards:** **Grievance** (a soul-forged greatsword; withers; right-click for a **Duel Lunge**), the **Seal of the Vow**, **Soul Embers** (regeneration, absorption, speed), netherite scrap and ancient debris, diamonds, a Totem of Undying.

### What the lovers say about the Wayfarer

Both of them knew her name, and neither could say how. The tablets say: *"I wrote it down once, in my own hand, and the ink ran. I think the Eclipse has been reading over my shoulder since the beginning."* The Eighth Voice, the page, the First Dawn ground into ink - the pieces are almost all in the player's journal now.

## 12. The dead of the high snow and the fen

| Creature | Place in the story |
|---|---|
| **Rimewalker** | Pilgrims who followed Ysolde's song into the snow and never turned back. The cold keeps them walking. |
| **Hoarbone** | The Choir's missing voices, taught to sing without breath. They carry frozen bows. |
| **Mirewalker** | Travellers the fen kept. Their light sometimes rises again. |
| **Lanternbone** | Morwen's lamp-bearers, still walking the path with their hooked poles. |
| **Mire Wisp** | The light itself. |

Their drops make better metal: **Rime Shards** forge **Rimesteel** (iron that remembers winter), **Glacial Shards** make **Glacite**, and **Wisp Essence** makes **Wispsteel**. A tablet in the fen says it best: *"Alloys of rime keep a blade sharp in the cold. Alloys of wisp-light keep a blade honest in the dark. Neither will keep you alive. Carry both."*

## 14. What happens next

The story is deliberately unfinished. The next Seals are not yet written. Things the journal already hints at, which later updates will explore:

- Why the Wayfarer's notes end after the Second Seal (she was still walking after the Fourth - Morwen saw her lamp - and after the Sixth: both lovers knew her name).
- What the Eighth Voice is, and why the Eclipse hungers for the page and not for the Shards.
- What the Seven Shards become if they are ever reunited.
- Whether a Keeper can be freed from a Seal without a death.
- Who the Eclipse was, before it was hungry.

## 15. For writers (how the lore is built so it can grow)

- **Seals and Keepers are the main line.** Each Seal gets a Keeper, a place, a fight with a *reason* (not just an obstacle), and a final plea or secret.
- **Side bosses are the Hollow Lords**: casualties of the Eclipse that give loot, relics and lore, never the main clue.
- **The journal is the only narrator.** Every clue is a Wayfarer quote followed by a plain instruction in a second colour.
- **Each chapter is data.** Adding a chapter means adding steps and lore pages in `tools/scripts/story.js` (see `CLAUDE.md`). Overworld roads live in `ROADS` and are walked in parallel; the main line is `STEPS`.
- **Every road pairs an exploration puzzle with a fight that tests the same idea** (the Cantor's rhythm, Morwen's light).
- **Keepers are tragedies, not villains.** The Eclipse is the villain; everyone else is paying for it.
