A story-driven adventure for Minecraft Bedrock. Follow the Wayfarer's Journal from a cherry-blossom shrine to a frozen cathedral and a drowned chapel in the fen, watch every great boss arrive in a cinematic, fight six main Keepers - two of them in the Nether and much harder - with their own fog, music and health bars, loot twenty-seven places, and keep the survival you love: new ores, stones, crops, animals and monsters - plus pearl stasis chambers and a claim block that works on any server.

Install in 30 seconds

  • Download EclipsedRealms_v{VER}.mcaddon from the releases folder.
  • Open the file - Minecraft imports the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.
  • Create a new world (or edit one and activate both packs). Requires Bedrock 1.26.50+, no experimental toggles.
  • Explore new chunks - ores, stones, crops, animals and structures only generate in terrain created after the pack is on.
Servers (Aternos, BDS...) - upload both packs and enable them for the world. Nobody has to be an operator: every system, including the Sanctum Warden, works for normal players.
