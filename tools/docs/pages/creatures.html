The Veiled Blossom new · Chapter I boss

Hanae, Keeper of the First Seal, is the first main boss of the story and a deliberately gentle, early-game fight (260 HP, small damage). She never wants to kill you - she wants you to turn back. If a player gets badly hurt she lowers her parasol and stops attacking until they recover.

  • How to reach her: not by chance. Follow the Wayfarer's Journal: find a Cherry Grove, thin the Hollow Court (3 Gravewalkers + 2 Cave Weavers), present the offerings (16 Pink Petals, Silk Gland, Hollow Bone, Spore Cluster) to receive the Veiled Mirror, find Hanae's Shrine and touch its Blossom Altar, then use the mirror on the altar at night.
  • Phase 1 - The Veil: fans of petal blades, a Mirror Step (she appears behind you after a shimmering warning) and Veiled Echoes - translucent reflections. While echoes live she is shielded; they shatter in one hit.
  • Phase 2 - Falling Blossoms: the fog thickens and turns petal-pink. Ringed targets mark where petals will burst; Blossom Vexes (small masked spirits, custom to this pack) harry you.
  • Phase 3 - Eclipse Bloom: her colours darken, the fog turns black-red and a slow petal spiral fills the air. She begs you to run.
  • Loot: the full Veilbloom Regalia (netherite-strength pink armour: Speed and Jump Boost set bonus), Veilbloom Shards (repair), the Seal of the Blossom, diamonds, an enchanted golden apple, Pink Petals.

Her story: read the lore (spoilers).

Ysolde, the Frost Cantor new · Road III boss

The Third Keeper conducts her fight in 3/4 time: every attack is announced by the beat (a harp note, strong on the downbeat). 520 HP, three phases, and she is the first boss where you are meant to move to a rhythm.

  • How to reach her: open Road III in the Wayfarer's Journal (after the First Seal). Read the four Verse Stones in snowy lands (the Cantor's Tuning Fork points you to them), find the Frozen Cathedral in the high peaks, strike its four chimes in the order of the sun's road, then strike the fork on the Cantor's Lectern.
  • Phase 1 - Overture: the Aria (fans of frozen notes), Resonance Rings that expand from her - jump them - and the Choir answers: Hoarbones and Rimewalkers.
  • Phase 2 - Crescendo: Icicle Chorus (marked circles that erupt) and the Whiteout, a blizzard that shoves you around until you kneel (sneak).
  • Phase 3 - Requiem: a double note spiral and the Final Verse: she channels for 3 seconds - hit her hard enough to break it (she is stunned), or run before the wave. It never takes you below 4 HP in one blow.
  • Loot: Frostsong, the Seal of the Choir, Glacial Shards, Rime Shards, diamonds, enchanted golden apple, amethyst.

Morwen, the Mire Lantern new · Road IV boss

The Fourth Keeper hangs lights over the fen. Her fight is about light: every few seconds the fen swallows the lights of everyone who is not carrying one. Hold a torch, lantern or glowstone and you keep your sight; the Keeper's Lantern lights everyone within 10 blocks. 560 HP.

  • How to reach her: open Road IV (after the Frost Cantor): gather 6 Wisp Essence and a Lantern for the Keeper's Lantern, find the Drowned Chapel in a swamp, read the Fen Notes (half in a fen hut, half in a flooded room reached through the chapel's pool), light the three sconces in the right order, then use the lantern on the Marsh Altar at night.
  • Phase 1 - Misdirection: foxfire bolts, Wisp Swarms that blind, and a lantern swing if you stand too close.
  • Phase 2 - Lost: Lantern Mirages - she vanishes among decoys; only the real one flickers with lantern-motes, and striking a mirage costs you your sight.
  • Phase 3 - Hollow Light: Lantern Rain (marked circles - half damage if lit) and a fog you cannot see through. Keep your own flame burning.
  • Loot: the Mirelight Scythe, the Seal of the Lantern, Wisp Essence, diamonds, netherite scrap, glow berries, enchanted golden apple.

Varenna, the Cinder Bride new · Road V boss · Nether

The Fifth Keeper - a bride in a burnt gown, a spiked crown, a halo of embers, a forge hammer and eight forged swords circling her. 900 HP, four phases, much harder than Roads III and IV. Her fight is a forge. Her Forged Blades () are the key: while any blade lives she is shielded (80% of your damage is healed back), and every blade a player cuts down parries her for 2.5% of her life.

  • How to reach her: open Road V (after Road IV; an Ember Compass appears). In the Forge of Vows make three offerings to the three pedestals in the order the vow was spoken (Gold Ingot, Blaze Rod, Ghast Tear - the tablets tell you; out of order burns you and returns your items) to get the Cinder Ring; find the Ashen Chapel with the compass, touch the Bridal Anvil, then set the ring on it.
  • Abilities: Anvil Slam (rings and ember geysers marked under you), Cinder Brand (the mark detonates on whoever stands near the branded player - spread out), Forge Heat (sectors of floor glow and then burn - the safe ones show no embers), the Last Dance (she crosses the floor and strikes from behind) and, in the last phase, the Wedding March (endless blades).
  • Loot: Vowbreaker (hammer, 15 dmg, ignites, right-click Anvil Slam), Seal of the Forge, Cinder Hearts, netherite scrap, ancient debris, diamonds, enchanted golden apples.

Corvane, the Ashen Groom new · Road VI boss · Nether

The Sixth Keeper - a top-hatted skeleton in a ruined tailcoat, a soul-fire heart, a greatsword with a wedding ring on its guard and the Bride's veil tied round his arm. 1100 HP, four phases, the hardest and fastest Keeper. His fight is a duel of stances, shown by the glow of his armour:

  • How to reach him: open Road VI (after Road V). Use the Ember Compass to find the Toll Tower in a Soul Sand Valley, touch its core and ring back the pattern of bells it tolls (3, 4, then 5 bells) to receive the Grave Key; find the Hollow Hall, touch the Grave Altar, then set the key in it.
  • Abilities: Duel Lunge (a telegraphed line), Soul Chains (drag you to him unless you break line of sight), Marrow Rain, Grave Echoes (wither skeletons) and the Widow's Toll (rings to jump). A hit his stance deflects is healed straight back - the action bar tells you why.
  • Loot: Grievance (greatsword, 14 dmg, withers, right-click Duel Lunge), Seal of the Vow, Soul Embers, netherite scrap, ancient debris, diamonds, a Totem of Undying.

Hollow Lords (side bosses) new

The Hollow King, Mosswood Colossus and Silkqueen are side bosses: casualties of the Eclipse that give loot, relics and journal lore pages but are not on the road of the Seals. Each needs a relic (see crafting reference), a ritual block, and the right conditions. If conditions are wrong you get a hint and keep the relic. Only one of each boss can be active near the ritual spot. All have a boss bar, three phases (at 66% and 33% health) and custom death messages.

Hollow King 450 HP

  • Summon: use a Grave Sigil on a Bone Block at night or below Y 48.
  • Abilities: Call the Court - raises Gravewalkers (and Revenants from phase 2), max 8 alive. Soul Drain - weakens, slows and damages every player within 16 blocks and heals the king. Bone Slam shockwave when you stand close. Phase changes unleash a Wail (knockback, blindness); the final phase adds Darkness. He also shoots Bone Shards at range.
  • Loot: Totem of Undying, Hollow Crown, Grave Dust, Hollow Bone, diamonds, emeralds, sometimes an enchanted golden apple.

Mosswood Colossus 650 HP

  • Summon: use a Verdant Heart on a Moss Block in daylight with the open sky above.
  • Abilities: huge ground slams, Root Snare (heavy slowness + poison on everyone near), spawns Sporelings. Bark Shield: while its sporelings are alive it takes far less damage - kill them first.
  • Loot: Heartwood Core (right-click: big absorption / regeneration / resistance), Spore Clusters, Moss Blocks, diamonds, an enchanted golden apple, maybe Netherite Scrap.

Silkqueen 400 HP

  • Summon: use a Silk Idol on a Cobweb below Y 45.
  • Abilities: Web Burst (cobwebs on you), Brood (Cave Weavers), Silk Tether (yanks distant players in), Web Bolts, leaping poison bites, Venom in the last phase. Webs she makes disappear after a while.
  • Loot: Silkqueen's Fang (venom sword), Silk Glands, lots of string, spider eyes, diamonds, ender pearls.

Tips

  • Fight the Hollow King near light and a shield; Colossus needs open ground; the Silkqueen is best fought with a sword that cuts webs fast (any sword does) and water buckets or milk for poison.
  • Trophy items: Hollow Crown goes in the helmet slot (night vision, enchantable, repairable with Hollow Bone). Silkqueen's Fang is enchantable and repairable with Silk Glands.

Nether & End (from v1)

  • Ashen Warg (Nether): melee + leap up close, spits Ember Spit when you keep your distance, ignites targets.
  • Voidweaver (End): teleporting sorcerer shooting Void Bolts.
  • Eclipse Sovereign (the second main boss, Chapter II): 1600 HP, 3 phases, slam shockwaves, Eclipse Orb volleys, summons Voidweavers. Craft an Eclipse Sigil (nether star + 4 Void Essence + 4 Pyrelite Ingot) and use it on an Eclipse Altar (End shrines, or craft one). Drops the Eclipse Core.
