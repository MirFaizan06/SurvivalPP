History: v1.5 shipped the calm piano piece "First Light Particles" (Yoiyami) for the Veiled Blossom, v1.6 shipped ambient loops by isaiah658 ("Ambient Relaxing Loop", "Heavenly Loop", "Echoes of the Past") for the Frost Cantor and the Mire Lantern; all three were replaced by real boss themes in v1.7 / v1.7.1 because they were beautiful but not boss-type.

The tracks were re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis (stereo, ~100-190 kbps) for the add-on. They live in `tools/assets/music/` and are copied into the resource pack by `tools/gen/gen_music.py`.
