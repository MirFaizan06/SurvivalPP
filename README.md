# Eclipsed Realms: Chronicles of the Seven Seals

**A story-driven adventure for Minecraft Bedrock - follow the Wayfarer's Journal, watch every great boss arrive in a cinematic, fight them with their own fog, music and health bars, loot twenty-three places, and survive with new ores, stones, crops, animals and monsters.**
Made by **Tech Bytes Design** · Current version **1.6.0** · Needs Minecraft Bedrock **1.26.50 or newer** · No experimental toggles required

📖 **Illustrated guide:** <https://mirfaizan06.github.io/SurvivalPP/> · 📜 **The full lore (spoilers):** [LORE.md](LORE.md) · 📋 **Changelog:** [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Download **`EclipsedRealms_v1.6.0.mcaddon`** from the [`releases/`](releases) folder.
2. Open it - Minecraft imports the Behavior Pack *and* the Resource Pack.
3. Create a **new world** (or edit an existing one and enable both packs). 
4. New terrain only: ores, structures, stones, crops and animals appear in chunks generated **after** the pack is enabled. Explore somewhere new.

> **Servers (Aternos, BDS, Realms-style hosts):** upload the same packs, enable both in the world, and make sure the server allows the `@minecraft/server` and `@minecraft/server-ui` script modules (hosts do by default). **Nobody needs to be an operator** - everything, including the Sanctum Warden, works for normal players.

## The story in one minute

Long ago the world had one sky and one dawn that never ended. Then the **Eclipse** - the Hollow Sun - swallowed the First Dawn and broke the world into three Realms and **Seven Shards**. Seven Keepers bound the Shards into **Seven Seals**, and slowly became the thing they guard.

When you first join you receive the **Wayfarer's Journal**, written by someone who walked this road before you. It gives you **one clue at a time**:

1. **Chapter I - The Veiled Blossom.** Find a Cherry Grove, thin the Hollow Court, gather offerings, find a hidden shrine and call **Hanae**, Keeper of the First Seal. She is a gentle early boss who never wanted to hurt you - she wanted you to turn back.
2. **Chapter II - The Second Seal.** The journey continues to the End and the **Eclipse Sovereign**, the second main boss.
3. **Road III - The Silent Choir.** After the First Seal the journal forks. Read four **Verse Stones** in the snow, strike the **chimes of the Frozen Cathedral** in the order of the sun's road, and face **Ysolde, the Frost Cantor**, who fights in 3/4 time.
4. **Road IV - The Lantern in the Mire.** Gather **Wisp Essence**, solve the sconce riddle of the **Drowned Chapel** (half of it is in a flooded room) and face **Morwen, the Mire Lantern**, whose fen swallows every light you do not carry.

The Hollow King, Mosswood Colossus and Silkqueen are **side bosses** (Hollow Lords): loot, relics and lore pages, but not on the main road. Read the whole history, with spoilers, in [LORE.md](LORE.md).

## How things work (quick guide)

### Ores & gear
* **Azurite** - Overworld, deep underground and in geodes. **Pyrelite** - Nether veins and lava geodes. **Eclipsite** - End stone.
* Break any of them with a **Netherite, Pyrelite or Eclipsite pickaxe** only. Silk Touch, Fortune and XP behave like vanilla ores.
* Smelt raw ore → ingot (furnace or blast furnace). Craft tools/armour with the usual patterns (Azurite uses sticks, Pyrelite blaze rods, Eclipsite end rods).
* Everything is **enchantable** (correct slots), **anvil / Mending repairable**, and Pyrelite/Eclipsite are fire-proof.

### Stones
| Stone | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Marlstone** (warm cream) | Everywhere underground + boosted in dripstone caves |
| **Slate** (blue-grey) | Boosted under mountains and high peaks |
| **Verdite** (mossy green) | Lush caves, jungles, swamps |
| **Umbrastone** (near-black) | Deep Dark and the deepest layers |

Each comes as block, polished, bricks, stairs, slabs and double slabs. Craft in a table or use a **stonecutter**.

### Trees
Plant the **sapling** on dirt/grass; give it light and space, or use **bone meal**. Leaves decay, shears / Silk Touch keep them. Wood blocks are fuel, carry the vanilla tags and **craft into everything vanilla wood does** (crafting table, chest, barrel, tools, bowls, bookshelf, charcoal, campfire…).

### Farming & food
* Seeds come from **wild plants** you find in the world (plains, jungle, savanna, desert, forest, taiga, meadow, hills) or from the produce itself (craft a Tomato → 2 seeds).
* Plant on **hydrated farmland** under light level 9+. Crops grow **4 stages**; **bone meal** works. **Right-click a ripe plant** to harvest *and replant in one click* (strawberries regrow). Fortune adds yield.
* Cook: Roasted Corn (smelt a cob), Tomato Soup, Oat Bread, Oat Porridge, Strawberry Pie, Chili Stew, Hunter's Stew - and cook venison / yak / pheasant in a furnace, smoker or campfire. Dishes give short buffs (fire resistance, regeneration, absorption, speed, haste…).

### Animals
**Meadow Deer** (forests, meadows, taiga), **Highland Yak** (mountains - right-click with a bucket for milk), **Wild Boar** (forests, jungle - *fights back, and its whole herd joins in*), **Pheasant** (plains, forests, savanna - sheds feathers). Breed with their favourite food (apples/oats, wheat/oats, carrots/corn, seeds). Drops: venison, yak meat, pork + **boar tusks**, pheasant, leather, feathers.

### Monsters
* **Gravewalker** - hunched undead in a burial shroud; weakens you, calls its pack when hurt, burns in daylight.
* **Revenant** - armoured skeleton; shoots slowing **bone shards**, switches to a blade when you get close, doesn't burn.
* **Cave Weaver** - big cave spider; poison bite, climbs walls and spits **webs** that slow you.
* **Sporeling** - small fungus; harmless-looking, but puffs **nausea spores** and bursts when killed.

### The Veiled Blossom (Chapter I)
Reach her through the journal, not by luck: **find a Cherry Grove → slay 3 Gravewalkers and 2 Cave Weavers → present 16 Pink Petals, a Silk Gland, a Hollow Bone and a Spore Cluster (you receive the Veiled Mirror) → find Hanae's Shrine and touch the Blossom Altar → at night, use the mirror on the altar.**

She is a **gentle early-game boss** (260 HP, small damage) with a unique fight: fans of petal blades, a *Mirror Step* that puts her behind you, shielding *Veiled Echoes*, custom *Blossom Vexes*, falling-blossom rings and a slow petal spiral - while the fog thickens and darkens. If you are badly hurt she lowers her parasol and **stops attacking**. She drops the **Veilbloom Regalia** (armour as strong as netherite, Speed + Jump Boost set bonus), Veilbloom Shards, the Seal of the Blossom and a Chapter II clue.

### Road III - Ysolde, the Frost Cantor
Open *Road III* in the journal (after the First Seal; you receive a **Cantor's Tuning Fork**). **Read all four Verse Stones** (snowy lands; the fork points to them) → find the **Frozen Cathedral** in the high peaks and strike its four **chimes** in the order of the sun's road (Dawn, Zenith, Dusk, Midnight - the chimes do not hang in that order) → strike the fork on the **Cantor's Lectern**. She fights in 3/4 time: **jump** her Resonance Rings, **kneel** against the whiteout, and **break the Final Verse** with damage. Drops **Frostsong**, the Seal of the Choir and Glacial Shards.

### Road IV - Morwen, the Mire Lantern
Opens when Road III is done. Present **6 Wisp Essence + a Lantern** for the **Keeper's Lantern** → find the **Drowned Chapel** in a swamp, read the **Fen Notes** (one half is in a fen hut, the other in a flooded room reached through the chapel's pool) and light the three sconces in the right order → at night, use the lantern on the **Marsh Altar**. In the fight **carry a light** (torch, lantern, glowstone; the Keeper's Lantern lights the whole party), watch for **Lantern Mirages**. Drops the **Mirelight Scythe** and the Seal of the Lantern.

### Alloy gear
Shapeless crafts, no ore hunt: **Rimesteel Ingot** = Iron + 2 Rime Shards, **Glacite Ingot** = Diamond + 2 Glacial Shards, **Wispsteel Ingot** = Gold + Netherite Scrap + 2 Wisp Essence. Full tool and 3D armour sets with set bonuses; see the guide.

### Hollow Lords (side bosses)
Each needs a **relic** (crafted from the monsters' drops), a **ritual block** and the **right conditions**. Using the relic with the wrong conditions tells you what is missing and keeps your relic.

| Boss | Relic | Use it on | Conditions | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hollow King** | Grave Sigil (4 Hollow Bone, 4 Grave Dust, Gold Block) | Bone Block | Night - or deep underground | Raises undead, drains life, wails in the final phase |
| **Mosswood Colossus** | Verdant Heart (4 Spore Cluster, 4 Moss Block, Emerald Block) | Moss Block | Daytime, open sky | Root snares, shield while its sporelings live |
| **Silkqueen** | Silk Idol (4 Silk Gland, 4 Cobweb, Diamond) | Cobweb | Below Y 45 | Web bursts, brood of Cave Weavers, silk tether pulls you in |

All three have boss bars, three phases and great loot: the Hollow King drops a **Totem of Undying** and the **Hollow Crown** (wear it: night vision), the Colossus drops the **Heartwood Core** (right-click: big absorption/regeneration burst), the Silkqueen drops **Silkqueen's Fang** (a venom sword). Relics also turn up (rarely) in structure chests.

### Structures
**Adventure structures** (new in 1.3) generate on their own in fresh chunks, like outposts and mansions - the harder the build, the better the loot:

| Difficulty | Structure | Where | Traps / guards | Loot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | **Bandit Lookout** | Plains, savanna, forest, meadow, taiga | none | tier 1 (2 chests) |
| Easy | **Sunken Well** | Plains, savanna, desert, forest, taiga | silverfish niche, webs | tier 1-2 (3 chests) |
| Medium | **Ruined Watch Keep** | Plains, forest, taiga, swamp, savanna | TNT plate, Gravewalker spawner | tier 2 (3 chests) |
| Medium | **Dune Vault** | Desert, mesa | fall-in shaft, 9-TNT plate, Husk spawner | tier 2-3 (5 chests) |
| Medium | **Thorn Shrine** | Jungle, swamp, mangrove | spike pit, wither roses, Cave Spider spawner | tier 3 (2 chests) |
| Medium | **Frost Cairn** | Frozen biomes | powder-snow pit, Stray spawner | tier 3 (2 chests) |
| Hard | **Highland Bastion** | Windswept hills, mountains | 3 TNT plates, 5 spawners, lava vault | tier 3-5 (9 chests) |
| Hard | **Sunken Crypt** | Underground, anywhere | 3 TNT plates, 3 spawners, lava moat | tier 4-5 (6 chests) |
| Hard | **Pyre Bastion** | Nether | lava ring, TNT plate, Warg + Wither Skeleton spawners | Nether tier (3 chests) |
| Hard | **Eclipse Vault** | The End | 3 TNT plates, Voidweaver + Endermite spawners | End tier (3 chests) |

Tips: a lone **stone pressure plate** hides TNT (dig around it), torches stop spawners, **Silk Touch** takes silverfish blocks safely, bring a **water bucket** for lava, **leather boots** for powder snow. Deep chests can hold boss relics, Totems of Undying and Elytra. Full details and plan renders: the [Adventure page](https://mirfaizan06.github.io/SurvivalPP/adventure.html).

**Smaller sites:** Forgotten Graveyard (crypt + ready-made Hollow King plinth), Verdant Shrine (Moss Block altar), Silk Nest (underground, altar cobweb), Hunter's Camp, Mossy Boulders.

### Stasis chambers (Java behaviour)
1. Craft a **Stasis Chamber** and a **Stasis Trigger Plate**.
2. Use an **Ender Pearl** on the chamber (it is bound to you; sneak + empty hand takes it back).
3. Use a **fishing rod aimed at the plate** to link it (sneak + use clears the link).
4. Anywhere, any dimension: **cast** = press the plate, **reel** = release. You teleport to the chamber, take pearl damage, the pearl is consumed.
Placing chambers/plates registers a ticking area automatically so they stay loaded.

### Sanctum Warden (protect your base)
**Crafting is intentionally brutal** - you need a boss drop:

```
N E N      N = Netherite Ingot      C = Eclipse Core (Eclipse Sovereign drop)
O C O      E = Eclipsite Ingot      T = Totem of Undying
R T R      O = Obsidian             R = Pyrelite Ingot
```
Place it and you are the owner. **Right-click** to configure: range (chunk or 8-48 blocks), intruder response (warn → repel → expel → seize items → kick), block & container protection, trusted players, boundary preview and a test mode. Craft **Sanctum Passes** (paper + azurite ingot + ender pearl) and attune them on the Warden to give friends access.

Works on servers **without any operators**:
* Owners manage everything themselves; only the owner can remove the Warden.
* Each player may keep **3** wardens (stops claim-spam).
* If an owner stays offline for **21 real days**, the zone lapses and the next player to right-click it may claim it - no admin needed.
* Operators and anyone tagged `ecr_sanctum_bypass` are exempt (optional).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Nothing new generates | Only **new chunks** get new content. Use a new world or travel to unexplored land. |
| Item won't craft in the 2x2 inventory grid | Update to v1.3.0 - single-ingredient recipes (log → planks…) were fixed. |
| Bosses won't summon | Read the on-screen hint: each needs the right block, time and height. |
| No structures found | They only generate in new chunks. Hard ones are rare (about 1 per 400 chunks); the Sunken Crypt is underground. |
| A creature is missing / `/summon` says not found | Run `/scriptevent ecr:diag` - it lists any creature the game failed to load. Then check Settings > Creator > Content Log and re-activate the Behavior Pack in the world. (v1.6.0 fixed the cause of the Gravewalker / Cave Weaver / Revenant / Sporeling / Wild Boar load failures.) |
| A boss intro cutscene misbehaves | `/scriptevent ecr:cinematic release` frees you; `/scriptevent ecr:cinematic off` disables the scenes. `/scriptevent ecr:bossbar off` hides the action-bar boss bar. |
| I lost my journal | Craft a new one (Book + Ink Sac + Pink Petals). Progress is saved on you, not on the book. |
| Scripts seem dead | Check the content log for `[Eclipsed Realms] ready`. A failed module is named there. |
