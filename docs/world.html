Ores & gear

OreWhereTier
AzuriteOverworld - deep underground, deepslate layers and geodesBetter than diamond (1850 durability)
PyreliteNether - molten veins and lava geodes, Pyre SpiresBetter than netherite (3600 durability, fire-proof)
EclipsiteThe End - end stone veins, Obelisks and Shrines4x netherite (8124 durability, mining speed 36, fire-proof)
  • All three can only be mined with a Netherite pickaxe or better (Pyrelite and Eclipsite pickaxes count). Silk Touch, Fortune and XP behave like vanilla ores.
  • Smelt raw ore in a furnace or blast furnace. Full tool and armour sets use the vanilla patterns (Azurite - stick, Pyrelite - blaze rod, Eclipsite - end rod).
  • Everything is enchantable with the right enchant slots, repairable in an anvil / with Mending, and carries on-hit effects (fire, wither, slowness). Wearing a full set gives set bonuses.

Armour & swords new look

Every tier has hand-built 3D armour (real geometry and painted textures - crystal crowns, horns and spikes, floating shards and a halo, blossom hairpins and a petal fan) and its own sword design: the Azurite crystal longsword, the Pyrelite molten falchion and the slim Eclipsite blade with a corona guard. Inventory icons are renders of the actual armour.

The Veilbloom Regalia (Veilbloom Crown, Haori, Hakama and Geta) drops from the Veiled Blossom. It matches netherite protection (3/8/6/3), is repaired with Veilbloom Shards, and a full set grants Speed I and Jump Boost I.

Stones new

They spawn as blobs inside normal stone, so you find them while caving and mining - not as a separate layer.

StoneLookWhere it concentrates
MarlstoneWarm creamEverywhere underground, boosted in dripstone caves
SlateBlue-greyUnder mountains and high peaks
VerditeMossy greenLush caves, jungles, swamps, mangrove swamps

Each has: block, polished, bricks, stairs, slabs and double slabs for polished and bricks. Crafting in a table (2x2 makes polished / bricks) or use the stonecutter for faster conversion and slabs/stairs.

  • Plant a sapling on dirt-type blocks with room and light; it grows over time or with bone meal. If there is no room, it tells you.
  • Each species has logs, stripped logs, wood, stripped wood, planks, stairs, slabs, fence, gate, trapdoor, door, button, pressure plate and leaves (decay, shears and Silk Touch keep them).
  • Wood is fuel, smelts into charcoal, and crafts into everything vanilla wood does: crafting table, chest, barrel, bowl, bookshelf, wooden tools, loom, lectern, campfire...

Farming new

CropFind wild inUse
TomatoPlains, jungleEat, Tomato Soup, Hunter's Stew
CornSavanna, plainsEat, Roasted Corn (smelt), feed boars and pheasants
StrawberryForests, birch, taiga, meadow, cherry groveEat, Strawberry Pie. The bush regrows
ChiliMesa, desert, savannaChili Pepper (eat any time), Chili Stew
OatsPlains, meadow, taiga, hillsOat Bread, Porridge, hay, feed yaks
  1. Find a wild plant and break it for seeds + produce (or craft seeds from any produce: Tomato → 2 seeds).
  2. Plant seeds on farmland. Crops need light level 9+ and grow much faster on hydrated farmland. If the farmland is removed the plant pops.
  3. 4 growth stages. Bone meal adds 1-2 stages.
  4. Right-click a ripe plant to harvest and replant in one click (breaking also works; Fortune adds yield).

Structures new

All generate in new chunks only. For the ten big loot-and-trap strongholds see the Adventure Structures page; these are the smaller decorative / ritual sites:

Forgotten Graveyard

Forests, plains, taiga, swamps. Fenced cemetery, slate gravestones, a crypt with a loot chest and a Bone Block plinth ready for the Hollow King ritual.

Verdant Shrine

Forests, taiga, jungle, meadows. A ring of mossy pillars around a Moss Block altar, wild crops and a chest.

Silk Nest

Underground, sealed in rock - mine into webs to find it. Altar cobweb, egg sacs and a chest.

Hunter's Camp

Forests, taiga, plains, meadows. Tent, campfire, drying rack, barrel and a chest of venison, arrows and leather.

Mossy Boulder

Forests, taiga, hills and mountains. Decoration made from moss, cobble and the new stones.

Rare relics (Grave Sigil, Verdant Heart, Silk Idol) can appear in the structure chests, so you can find a boss summon instead of crafting it.
