Overworld bosses new

Each boss needs a relic (see crafting reference), a ritual block, and the right conditions. If conditions are wrong you get a hint and keep the relic. Only one of each boss can be active near the ritual spot. All have a boss bar, three phases (at 66% and 33% health) and custom death messages.

Hollow King 450 HP

  • Summon: use a Grave Sigil on a Bone Block at night or below Y 48.
  • Abilities: Call the Court - raises Gravewalkers (and Revenants from phase 2), max 8 alive. Soul Drain - weakens, slows and damages every player within 16 blocks and heals the king. Bone Slam shockwave when you stand close. Phase changes unleash a Wail (knockback, blindness); the final phase adds Darkness. He also shoots Bone Shards at range.
  • Loot: Totem of Undying, Hollow Crown, Grave Dust, Hollow Bone, diamonds, emeralds, sometimes an enchanted golden apple.

Mosswood Colossus 650 HP

  • Summon: use a Verdant Heart on a Moss Block in daylight with the open sky above.
  • Abilities: huge ground slams, Root Snare (heavy slowness + poison on everyone near), spawns Sporelings. Bark Shield: while its sporelings are alive it takes far less damage - kill them first.
  • Loot: Heartwood Core (right-click: big absorption / regeneration / resistance), Spore Clusters, Moss Blocks, diamonds, an enchanted golden apple, maybe Netherite Scrap.

Silkqueen 400 HP

  • Summon: use a Silk Idol on a Cobweb below Y 45.
  • Abilities: Web Burst (cobwebs on you), Brood (Cave Weavers), Silk Tether (yanks distant players in), Web Bolts, leaping poison bites, Venom in the last phase. Webs she makes disappear after a while.
  • Loot: Silkqueen's Fang (venom sword), Silk Glands, lots of string, spider eyes, diamonds, ender pearls.

Tips

  • Fight the Hollow King near light and a shield; Colossus needs open ground; the Silkqueen is best fought with a sword that cuts webs fast (any sword does) and water buckets or milk for poison.
  • Trophy items: Hollow Crown goes in the helmet slot (night vision, enchantable, repairable with Hollow Bone). Silkqueen's Fang is enchantable and repairable with Silk Glands.

Nether & End (from v1)

  • Ashen Warg (Nether): melee + leap up close, spits Ember Spit when you keep your distance, ignites targets.
  • Voidweaver (End): teleporting sorcerer shooting Void Bolts.
  • Eclipse Sovereign (boss): 1600 HP, 3 phases, slam shockwaves, Eclipse Orb volleys, summons Voidweavers. Craft an Eclipse Sigil (nether star + 4 Void Essence + 4 Pyrelite Ingot) and use it on an Eclipse Altar (End shrines, or craft one). Drops the Eclipse Core.
